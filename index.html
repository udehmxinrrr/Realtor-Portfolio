I have been in Real Estate for 12 Years.


Buying or selling a home is more than a transaction — it's one of life's biggest moments, and I feel honored to be part of it. I got into real estate because I genuinely love helping people find not just a house, but a place they can call home. I know this community well — the neighborhoods, the schools, the little local spots that make a place feel special. My goal is to make the process feel less overwhelming and more like something we're navigating together, with honesty, patience, and a lot of care along the way. Whether you're searching for your first home, your forever home, or getting ready to sell, I'm here to listen first and guide you with your best interests at heart. Let's find what's right for you.
